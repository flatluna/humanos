# Engrams Studio (Capability Studio) — Quick Overview

**Engrams Studio** (a.k.a. **Capability Studio**, `http://localhost:3010`) is the **authoring/AI-factory app** of Human OS — where instructional designers and HR admins turn raw source material into full courses.

**In a few words:** you upload a PDF/document, and a pipeline of AI agents automatically:
1. Extracts and chapters the raw content.
2. Builds a knowledge graph of concepts/entities and how they connect.
3. Designs the pedagogy for each section (Hypothesis → Teaching → Deep Dive → Recall → Production → Assessment) — the same "Memory Paradox" model used everywhere in Human OS.
4. Generates supporting media (illustrations, diagrams, short teaching videos).

The output is an **Engram-based course** (Structured Learning document or Capability Graph) that students then take in the real learning apps (human-os-web / human-os-employee), with voice/text AI tutors grounded strictly in that generated content.

Engrams Studio is **author-only** — it never carries real student progress data; it's where courses are built and reviewed before being published to real learners.
